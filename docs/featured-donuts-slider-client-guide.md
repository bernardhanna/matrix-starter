# Homepage Donut Slider — Editing Guide

This is the big black sliding banner on the homepage that shows your featured donuts, one at a time, with the photo, description, allergens, and an "Order Now" button.

This guide explains how to change what appears in it. You don't need any technical knowledge — everything is done in the normal WordPress admin area.

---

## Quick overview

There are **two places** to edit:

1. **The donut itself** (its photo, description, colour, allergens) — edited on the product page.
2. **The homepage** — where you choose *which* donuts appear in the slider and *in what order*.

---

## 1. Change the order, add, or remove donuts in the slider

This is the most common change.

1. Log in to WordPress.
2. In the left-hand menu, go to **Pages**.
3. Open the **Home** page (it may be labelled "Home" or "Front Page") and click **Edit**.
4. Scroll down to the **Featured Donuts** tab/section.
5. You'll see a list called **Featured Donuts** with the donuts currently in the slider.
   - **To add a donut:** click into the box, start typing the donut's name, and select it from the list.
   - **To remove a donut:** click the small **×** next to its name.
   - **To reorder them:** click and drag a donut up or down. The top one shows first.
6. Click **Update** (blue button, top right) to save.
7. Visit the homepage and refresh to see the change.

> The little number on each slide (e.g. **2 / 6**) updates automatically based on how many donuts you've added.

---

## 2. Edit a single donut's details

Each donut in the slider is a **product**. To change its photo, description, background colour, allergens, or thumbnail:

1. In the left-hand menu, go to **Products**.
2. Find and open the donut you want to edit.
3. Update any of the following:

| What you want to change | Where to change it |
|--------------------------|--------------------|
| **Main photo** (large image on the slide) | The **Product image** box (right-hand side) |
| **Donut name** | The title field at the top |
| **Description** | The main content/description box |
| **Background colour** of the slide | The **Donut Fields** tab → **Featured Donut Background Color** → *Select Color* |
| **Round thumbnail** (the little circle dot) | The **Donut Fields** tab → **Thumbnail Image** |
| **Allergens** shown on the slide | The **Donut Fields** tab → **Allergens** (type to add, click **×** to remove) |

4. Click **Update** to save.

---

## 3. Add a new allergen (if it's not in the list)

The allergens (Milk, Eggs, Nuts, etc.) each have their own little icon.

1. In the left-hand menu, go to **Allergens**.
2. Click **Add New**.
3. Give it a name (e.g. "Sesame").
4. Set its icon using the **Featured image** box (this small image is what shows next to the name on the slide).
5. Click **Publish**.
6. The new allergen is now available to select on any donut (see section 2).

---

## Tips

- **Always click Update / Publish** after making a change, then refresh the homepage to check it.
- **Photos:** use a good-quality square-ish image for the main donut photo. The round thumbnail should be a small, clear image of the donut.
- **Background colour:** pick a colour that matches the donut and lets the black text stay readable.
- If something doesn't appear, double-check the donut has been added to the **Featured Donuts** list on the Home page (section 1).

---

*If anything looks broken or won't save, contact your developer — the slider's layout and behaviour are controlled in the theme and don't normally need changing.*
