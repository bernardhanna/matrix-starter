Homepage hero slider — image size guide
=======================================

Right panel image (desktop) and top strip image (mobile) for hero slides.
Edit in WP Admin: Pages → Homepage → Hero tab → each slide → Right panel.

How images are displayed
------------------------
- Images use object-fit: cover (fill the box; edges may crop).
- Keep the main subject centred so cropping looks intentional.
- If no mobile image is uploaded, the desktop image is used on mobile (often crops badly on the short mobile strip).

Display sizes (on screen)
-------------------------
Desktop (1084px and up)
  Field:  Right image (desktop)
  Box:    50% of viewport width × 589px fixed height
  Layout: Right half of the hero
  Example at 1440px wide: ~720 × 589px

Mobile (under 1084px)
  Field:  Right image (mobile)
  Box:    Full width × 268px fixed height
  Layout: Top strip above the text panel
  Example at ~402px wide: ~402 × 268px (Figma reference)

Recommended upload sizes
------------------------
Desktop — Right image (desktop)
  Ideal:     1440 × 1180 px  (≈ 1.22:1, matches Figma at 1440px)
  Retina:    1920 × 1200 px  (safe for large screens)
  Ratio:     Landscape, roughly 5:4 to 3:2
  Format:    JPG or WebP, compressed for web

Mobile — Right image (mobile)
  Ideal:     828 × 552 px     (2× for ~414px-wide phones)
  Safe max:  1200 × 800 px    (3:2)
  Ratio:     Landscape, roughly 3:2 (≈ 1.5:1)
  Format:    JPG or WebP

Quick reference
---------------
Field                    On-screen target          Recommended file
Right image (desktop)    ~720×589 to ~960×589      1920 × 1200 px
Right image (mobile)     full width × 268px        1200 × 800 px

Composition tips
----------------
- Mobile: Place subject in the middle third vertically; top and bottom are cropped in the 268px band.
- Desktop: Slightly wider compositions work; the panel grows on large monitors (up to ~50% of viewport).
- Avoid very tall/portrait images unless the subject is centred; cover will crop heavily.

Related ACF fields (same slide)
-------------------------------
- Title image (desktop) / Title image (mobile) — left panel neon or graphic
- Background pattern (desktop) / Background pattern (mobile) — left panel repeat
- Heading, subtext, button — left panel copy (not the right image)
